|[2610.37646v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37646v1)|Measuring the magnetization profiles of galaxy groups and clusters|Kritti Sharma, Vikram Ravi, Elisabeth Krause|We combine RM2 stacks from SPICE-RACS around DESI galaxy groups and ROSAT clusters with optical depth profiles constrained jointly by the FRB DM-redshift relation and the kSZ effect, and obtain 95% upper limits on the rms line-of-sight magnetic field of <3.9-11.6 μG (<2.3-6.4 μG) in groups (clusters) for a 10~kpc coherence length. Combining these with thermal pressure profiles inferred from tSZ-shear cross-correlations, we obtain 95% lower limits on the plasma β of >0.31 (>11) in groups (clusters). Although the interpretation of current measurements remains challenging owing to depolarization along turbulent halo sightlines and the unknown coherence length, larger samples and DSA/Square Kilometer Array observations may resolve these uncertainties by measuring polarization-fraction profiles and the RM structure function using extended radio sources |T2.3 |
